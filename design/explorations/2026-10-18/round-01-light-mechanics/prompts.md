# Ferramenta: light mechanics — homepage concepts

Built-in Imagegen. Created 2026-10-18. Ten final concept images; no application source changed.

## Shared generation brief

Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: a high-fidelity desktop homepage design exploration for the Ferramenta family of Rust developer tools.
Primary request: Generate ONE complete, polished, realistic website screenshot, not a moodboard. Portrait canvas approximately 1440 wide by 1960 tall, representing a desktop browser width, all sections large and readable, from site header down to a compact footer. No browser chrome, no laptop mockup, no external concept title or numbered board.
Art direction: light theme ONLY, including header, footer and all code panels. Warm, precise, durable, carefully engineered German industrial design qualities expressed through proportion, restraint and details. Lightweight mechanical craft, fine tolerances, not heavy industry. Use warm white and light neutral surfaces, natural pale wood and finely brushed silver metal very selectively. Text mostly on plain light surfaces. Excellent contrast and generous spacing. Restrained regular or medium typography, sentence case headings, no oversized heavy condensed capitals. Material should look authentic but never turn the whole UI into a physical prop.
Product truth: Ferramenta is a family of independent Rust engines. Ferromark is a Markdown renderer for Rust and Node.js: Markdown to HTML plus front matter, heading IDs, footnotes, CommonMark and GFM; Rust and Node have distinct documented trust defaults, so do not claim all output is sanitized by default. Ferroni is Oniguruma's regex engine in memory-safe Rust, supporting lookbehind, backreferences, recursion, Unicode and the TextMate multi-pattern scanner. Ferriki runs on Ferroni; Ferromark pairs with Ferriki. Never portray all family members as a dependency pipeline.
Constraints: all visible interface copy in accurate US English. Realistic helpful content, concise copy, no lorem ipsum, no invented customer logos, testimonials, awards, download statistics, fabricated benchmark numbers, certifications, flags, or claims of origin. Small unobtrusive Ferramenta family link in the header and quiet family links near the footer. No dark sections, dark code editors, black slabs, massive gears, anvils, heavy bolts or riveted steel plates, rusty/grunge textures, distressed wood, steampunk, industrial violence, oversized badges, shiny chrome, orange gradients or generic card grids. No theme switcher. Compose a usable developer homepage, with real navigation, product explanation, code or output, proof links and an entry action.
