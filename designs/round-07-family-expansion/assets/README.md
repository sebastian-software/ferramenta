# Five-engine asset kit

Each product directory contains an untouched built-in Imagegen hero graphic and rendered icon master. Both have genuine alpha transparency. The mark is deliberately simpler than the instrument, and its broad shapes remain recognizable at 32–64px.

## Exports

- Transparent mark PNGs at 32, 48, 64, 128, 256 and 512px.
- A favicon ICO with 32/48/64px frames; no 16px frame.
- A true typeset horizontal wordmark in Helvetica Neue, supplied as transparent PNG.
- An opaque 180px Apple touch icon on the light family ground.

Use the mark at 40px beside a real wordmark with a 12px gap, and at 32px in a footer or favicon. Keep its original steel, graphite and oxide colors. Preserve the alpha channel and aspect ratio. Keep its silhouette clear against a light ground.

The hero instrument belongs in the large opening illustration. It is not the favicon. Similar materials and shared physical logic connect the two forms.

The shared paper and pewter texture masters are copied from the approved round-six kit, with source provenance retained. Source copy, exact prompts and original repository evidence live at the review root.

Run build-assets.mjs from the review root to reproduce the exports. The exporter only normalizes transparent margins, resizes, typesets the wordmark and packs favicon frames. It does not redraw or edit the original icon material.
