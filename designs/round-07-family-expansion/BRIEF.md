# Family expansion: five engine identities

The approved warm editorial direction extends to Ferriki, Ferrolex, Ferrocat, Ferralk and Ferrugo. Ferrovia and the independently branded applications are outside this round.

The appearance authority is the combined Ferromark/Ferroni homepage direction (39/40): warm beige paper, matte pewter, rust-brown oxide, dark warm ink, a readable grotesk, large gutters, and a narrow left introductory margin beside each main story. Light theme only. Each homepage has one substantial hero instrument and a lower body carried by prose, worked examples, tables and clear evidence.

Each product gets a transparent instrument graphic, a deliberately simpler rendered icon with 32/48/64px favicon support, a true typeset wordmark, and a complete homepage comp. The new icons follow the existing rendered steel/graphite/oxide icon family. Do not alter shipped icons or production packages.

Palette: paper #F4F0E6; reading inlay #FBFAF6; pewter #DCDDD6; ink #29362F; secondary ink #5C655B; oxide #88492F. Keep small text on calm light reading fields. Hero instruments use satin steel, warm oxide and graphite, precise finishing and restrained hardware.
