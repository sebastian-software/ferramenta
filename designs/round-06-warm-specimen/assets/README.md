# Assets for the warm editorial design

The document register is the Ferromark instrument: source and rendered document aligned by a registration bridge, with a separate metadata leaf. The pattern comparator is the Ferroni instrument: parallel pattern cassettes, a source ribbon and a carriage locating the next match.

The transparent instrument PNGs are copied from the round-three asset masters. The two logo folders contain the built-in Imagegen masters, raster wordmarks, 32–512px exports, ICO favicons and Apple touch icons from the shared logo kit. Each raster keeps its provenance sidecar. Use the mark at 32px or larger in this direction; preserve its transparency and aspect ratio.

The archive-wide original prompts remain in `../../logos/prompts.md` and `../../round-03-core-pages/prompts.md`. No image has been regenerated or edited while copying this asset kit.

## Material masters

`paper-master.png` supplies a very light warm ivory paper ground with fine fibers. `pewter-master.png` supplies a wide, light matte-metal surface with restrained finishing grain and physical illumination. Both were created with built-in Imagegen from the approved homepage material appearance. Their exact prompts, dimensions and hashes are recorded in adjacent sidecars and the root prompt bundle.

Use texture at restrained contrast and keep code and small data on calm light reading fields. The full-page designs remain the appearance authority. The material masters are supporting bitmap assets, not production CSS.
