# Warm editorial specimen

This is the approved combined design direction for both products, extended to all eight core pages. The layout and material inputs have separate, explicit roles. The approved homepages are images 39 and 40; the six new subpages use them as the visual family authority.

## Layout authority

Ferromark: round four, image 31. Ferroni: round four, image 32. These are the Working specimen pages with a narrow left margin column containing short introductory text for each story. A generous gutter separates that margin from the wider reading and evidence column. This identifying structure must remain visible throughout the page.

Round-four images 29 and 30 are not the selected layout. They were used in the first material studies. Those studies remain useful as material references.

## Material authority

Round-five Paper & pewter, images 35 and 36: warm ivory paper with a restrained fine fiber texture, matte pewter, dark warm ink, and rust-brown oxide accents. Materials, texture illumination, table treatment and color are applied to the selected margin-column layout. The full page must communicate the combined result.

Palette roles: paper #F4F0E6, inlay #FBFAF6, pewter #DCDDD6, ink #29362F, muted #5C655B, accent #88492F.

## Preserve

Keep the generous spacing, small left introductions, meaningful single hero object, working examples, complete source-backed content and airy tables. Broad material grounds support whole stories. The pewter measurement spread includes both the margin note and the main evidence column. Keep the same column geometry across the surface transition. Small text remains comfortably legible on calm light areas. Keep the document-register instrument for Ferromark and the pattern comparator for Ferroni. Light theme only.

## Avoid

Do not import the material-reference layout. No full-width body content beginning at the outer left margin, loss of the margin introductions, equal-width editorial columns, extra body illustrations, dark bands, dirty texture, decorative color washes or thick horizontal story dividers. Textures are a material system, not scattered embellishments.

## Evidence and scope

The round-four source copy and round-three evidence snapshots remain authoritative. These are built-in Imagegen raster studies, with exact prompts and provenance. Production components and accepted design records are unchanged. Local files only; no commit or staging.

## Eight-page family

Each product has Overview, Start with Rust, a core-function explainer, and Benchmarks. Similar content shares visual rules: installation commands, source/result specimens, API reference tables, trust notes, measurement figures and next-step links. The shared header, product mark, grotesk typography, paper, pewter and oxide make the family recognizable throughout.

The homepage uses short introductory notes in a narrow left margin. Documentation pages retain the useful narrow navigation and on-page outline from the earlier accepted subpages, with a generous main reading and code column. They do not add a second competing margin rail. The rendering explainer can use editorial margin introductions where its structure benefits from them.

Keep code and data on calm light inlays. Align table numbers on decimals or the right edge, prose labels on the left. A broad light pewter figure carries important measured evidence; ordinary reading stays on warm paper. Use generous story spacing and wide gutters. Actual ordered procedures may use quiet step cues; ordinary prose needs no numbered decoration.

## Reusable asset kit

The local assets directory contains the transparent document-register and pattern-comparator masters, both complete logo/favicon export kits, and reusable warm-paper and matte-pewter texture masters. Preserve provenance sidecars. Texture masters are supporting materials; the complete page comps remain the appearance reference.

Copy snapshots for all eight pages are in content/. Frozen benchmark evidence is in evidence/. Exact prompts and reference roles are in prompts.md and the manifest. The review generator and shared review stylesheet keep the local comparison views consistent; they are not the production website.
