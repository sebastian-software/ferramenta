# Material and color directions

[Open the paired gallery](index.html).

This round explores colors, textured materials, light, reading grounds, tables and evidence figures on the Editorial folio composition. Its layout differs from the selected target.

**Preferred material:** warm Paper & pewter. [Round six](../round-06-warm-specimen/index.html) combines it with the selected Working specimen layout, identified by the narrow left introductory column throughout.

- **Paper & pewter:** warm uncoated paper, satin pewter, graphite-green ink and oxide.
- **Porcelain & titanium:** mineral white, pale titanium, blue graphite and warm oxide.

Each direction includes both Ferromark and Ferroni. The meaningful document-register and pattern-comparator hero objects remain. No extra body illustrations are added.

[The brief](BRIEF.md) records the palettes and material intent. [The exact prompt set](prompts.md) and each PNG sidecar record built-in Imagegen provenance. [The manifest](manifest.json) links the layout actually used, final images, viewers and source copy. Original prompts are retained verbatim; their selection wording is superseded by the explicit layout references in round six.

These are raster design studies. The round-four source copy and recorded evidence remain the authority for API behavior, claims, text and numbers. Generated glyphs may differ.

The HTML baselines remain free of the rejected decorative backgrounds. This round does not implement a material system in the production site. Everything remains local and uncommitted.
