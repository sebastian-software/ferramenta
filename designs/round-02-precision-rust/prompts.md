## Shared brief

Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high-fidelity desktop homepage design for the Ferramenta family of Rust-native developer engines, round two.
Primary request: ONE finished, believable desktop website screenshot on a portrait canvas, approximately 1440px wide by 1960px high, header through footer, text large enough to inspect. No browser chrome, device mockup, concept title or externally numbered presentation board.
Art direction: LIGHT THEME ONLY. Elegant precision mechanics in clean brushed steel, satin stainless metal, tiny meticulously machined countersunk screws and restrained warm rust / iron-oxide details. Fine instrument making, not a steelworks. Stainless silver and warm oxide orange-brown are the identity; wood can be absent. Refined mechanical richness, thin cross sections and fine tolerances, generous warm white space, authentic miniature material details. Use rust as a clean stabilized oxide finish, very low-contrast even patina or tiny accent inlay; not flakes or dirt. Mostly flat white reading surfaces, subtle material only where composition calls for it. No heavy metal frames around every UI panel. Two to four tiny screws in mechanically meaningful locations, never scattered decoration.
Rust emphasis: Rust is the primary runtime, not a footnote. Product definition, primary action "Start with Rust", a prominent real "cargo add" installation line and a concise "Rust-native" marker. Keep this a developer product page. No fake safety certification. Rust branding can be a small tasteful rust-colored word or simple cog-and-R emblem; avoid cartoon mascots.
Typography: regular to medium width industrial humanist sans for headings, booklike clarity for body text, restrained monospace for literal code. Sentence case. Airy hierarchy, mostly regular and medium weight, no giant bold condensed capitals.
Product truth, instructions only, never visible copy: Ferromark renders Markdown to HTML for Rust and Node.js with CommonMark and GFM, front matter, heading IDs and footnotes; trust defaults differ by runtime, so make no blanket sanitized-by-default claim. Ferroni continues the Oniguruma regex engine in memory-safe Rust, supports lookbehind, backreferences, recursion and Unicode, plus the TextMate scanner. Ferriki runs on Ferroni and pairs with Ferromark; family engines are independent, never draw all family members as one pipeline.
Constraints: interface copy in US English. Do not reproduce art directions or instructions in visible copy. Use supplied concise copy and exact small specimens, no fabricated methods, API imports, benchmark figures, user logos, testimonials, awards, versions, ratings or certification. Light header, light footer, light code. No dark bands, black steel slabs, heavy industrial machinery, hammers, anvils, flames, molten material, sharp aggressive gear teeth, grunge, dirt, flaking corrosion, distressed wood, thick bolts, thick riveted plates, steampunk pipes, chrome glare, orange gradients, repeated generic cards or theme switcher. A quiet Ferramenta family link and real-looking Docs / GitHub navigation are required.
