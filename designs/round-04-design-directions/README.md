# Whole-page design directions

Open [the paired review](index.html). Six complete homepage concepts explore three coherent directions for Ferromark and Ferroni.

**Selected composition:** Working specimen, images 31 and 32, with the narrow left introductory column throughout. [Round six](../round-06-warm-specimen/index.html) combines this layout with the warm Paper & pewter material system. Round five used Editorial folio and serves as a material reference.

- **Editorial folio:** asymmetric magazine composition; open, typographic tables.
- **Working specimen:** large worked examples organize the page; annotations and specimen ledgers carry technical detail.
- **Measured narrative:** warm long-form reading paired with broad evidence spreads and designed comparison tables.

Each image has an exact built-in Imagegen prompt and provenance sidecar. [The brief](BRIEF.md) records the constraints and differences. [The prompt set](prompts.md) contains every final prompt. [The manifest](manifest.json) maps concepts, products, references and source copy.

The images are visual studies. [Ferromark source copy](content/ferromark.md) and [Ferroni source copy](content/ferroni.md), together with the round-three evidence snapshots, remain the text and numeric authority. Small generated glyphs may differ.

The previous [Ferromark HTML baseline](../homepages/ferromark.html) and [Ferroni HTML baseline](../homepages/ferroni.html) now have no decorative section backgrounds. Their broader layout is frozen while design exploration resumes. Production code and the six approved subpage images are unchanged. Everything remains local and uncommitted.
