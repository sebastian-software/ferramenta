# Ferramenta: the family entrance

Create the shared homepage and a distinct rendered logo for Ferramenta. Continue the approved warm editorial specimen direction from rounds 06 and 07. This is a local design exploration, not a production-site or shared-package migration.

## Purpose

Help a developer understand the family and choose the engine that fits the work. Show exactly the seven registered engines: Ferroni, Ferriki, Ferromark, Ferrolex, Ferrocat, Ferralk and Ferrugo. Each remains independently adoptable. Registry relations may be explained in prose but never drawn as a required pipeline. Applications and Ferrovia are outside this page's scope.

The current registry is the authority for names, descriptions, evidence, relations and destinations. Copy snapshots and asset provenance accompany the design. Avoid version numbers, invented benchmarks, download counts, unsupported compatibility or a family-wide safety guarantee.

## Visual plan

Palette: paper #F4F0E6, reading inlay #FBFAF6, pewter #DCDDD6, warm ink #29362F, secondary ink #5C655B, oxide #88492F. Light theme only. Warm fine-fiber paper and matte pewter textures carry the material variation. No decorative color gradients or dark sections.

Type: Helvetica Neue, with Helvetica/Arial fallbacks, matching the approved readable grotesk. A large product name, a smaller descriptive headline, relaxed body leading, and restrained technical labels. Align all reading content left. Keep paragraphs at readable measures.

Layout: one split hero, then the narrow left introductory margin beside broad editorial content. A complete seven-engine index combines real rendered icons, useful descriptions and destinations. Follow with a compact task-to-engine selection table and a prose-led workshop section. Use generous vertical intervals and horizontal gutters. Small table rules may clarify rows; page-wide divider lines and repeated large illustrations are unnecessary.

## Graphic and logo meaning

Hero: a shallow, open precision register holding seven individually removable tool inserts in separate parallel sockets. These share a measured mounting standard but have distinct broad working profiles: a pattern rail, token comb, document plate, letterform tile, paired catalog leaves, branching path and preview aperture. This is a collection, with no arrows, conveyor or mandatory order. Satin steel, warm graphite and one restrained oxide latch; no heavy industrial drama or clutter.

Mark: a compact F-shaped satin-steel registration fixture on a graphite backing, with two broad open keyed bays and one oxide insert. Its silhouette remains recognizable at 32px and distinct from the seven engine icons. The F-shaped common register represents Ferramenta's shared craft and independent tools. Use few major shapes, no microscopic screws or seven tiny symbols. Typeset the actual Ferramenta wordmark separately.

## Review against the brief

The inherited beige/oxide palette is deliberate and explicitly approved. The design's distinguishing features are the functional tool register, the true family icon set, the left introductory margin and the useful catalog content. A generic SaaS feature grid or fictitious cross-product performance chart would obscure the choice this page needs to support. Lower sections therefore use text and a selection table, not more hero graphics.

Deliver a complete Imagegen page comp, transparent hero and mark masters, a real wordmark, 32/48/64px favicon exports, a linked local preview, exact prompts and frozen registry evidence. Keep all changes local and uncommitted under designs/.
